excerpt: "This game is joyous and quite beautiful. It was my first real experience playing an Atelier game, but this time around I did not completely fail. I think most characters are quite charming, and the gameplay loop gets quite addictive. I am glad the series kind of figured itself out along the way, but I appreciate the obscurité of unlocking certain stories. 

I think this series is a great example for when achievements can hurt your experience playing a game. This game in particular prides itself on letting you experience a different story with different events if you replay it, yet that goes against the philosophy of getting as many cheevos in one go as possible. I am glad I played this on my Switch. The endgame is BRUTAL, and I would have not expected these games to be harder than a lot of current JRPG-style games."
