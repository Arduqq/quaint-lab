How filthy it made me feel to adore Pokopia. Aside from the very compelling building systems and great mix between Viva Pinata, Pokémon and (actually) Animal Crossing, I found it so easy to fall into a sense of community when checking on the different regions. The game thrives when it gets to show you something new. Any new friend, any new item, any new cranny of the world makes me feel curious and excited. They also manage to strike a great balance for people who browse too much Pinterest and people who are here to goof around.
